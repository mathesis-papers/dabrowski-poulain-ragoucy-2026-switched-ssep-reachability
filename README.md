# Switching reachability in twisted exclusion processes: extensions of Dabrowski, Poulain d'Andecy and Ragoucy's sector classification

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Extending Dabrowski, Poulain d'Andecy and Ragoucy's sector classification (Propositions 4.9, 4.10 and 4.14, pp. 13–15; arXiv:2602.18204v2, 11 June 2026), we answer their switching question (Section 7, p. 22): for a ring of at least three sites, alternating two twisted symmetric exclusion processes reaches every configuration precisely when the derived subgroup of the group generated by their twists acts transitively on the local states. For two sites, the criterion is instead transitivity of an explicitly generated subgroup on ordered pairs. The proof combines independent coordinate commutators with a conserved abelian charge. For three or more sites we classify all components, without a transitivity assumption, by the orbit profile and a charge in an explicit quotient of the twist group's abelianization. The method also covers finite families of twists. Norris's positive-time support theorem (Theorem 3.2.1, p. 111) gives the same reachability for positive dwell times; periodic switching converges to the uniform law on each component.
