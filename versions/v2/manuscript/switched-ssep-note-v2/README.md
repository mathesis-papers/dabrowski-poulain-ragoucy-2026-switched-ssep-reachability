# Switched twisted exclusion processes

The note answers the switching question of Mathieu Dabrowski, Loïc Poulain d'Andecy and Eric Ragoucy, *Twisted symmetric exclusion processes and set-theoretical R-matrices*, Section 7, p. 22 of arXiv:2602.18204v2.

For `L >= 3`, full switching reachability is equivalent to transitivity of the derived subgroup of the group generated by the two twists. For `L = 2`, it is equivalent to transitivity of the group generated by `(f,f^-1)` and `(g,g^-1)` on ordered pairs. The note classifies all components for arbitrary twist groups by orbit profile and abelian charge, extending the same authors' single-twist sector classification (Propositions 4.9, 4.10 and 4.14). The support results also hold for positive dwell times and fair schedules of finitely many twists.

## Exact finite checks

Requirements: Python 3.10 or newer, standard library only.

```sh
python3 verify.py --output verification-reproduced.json
cmp verification.json verification-reproduced.json
```

The program enumerates all 617 ordered pairs of permutations on alphabets of sizes 1 through 4, at lattice sizes 2, 3 and 4. It compares independently generated configuration components with the permutation-group criteria and checks the two-site stabilizer identities, rational branching probabilities and alternating supports. At three or more sites it checks the complete profile-and-charge fibers, counts and sizes for every twist group, including nontransitive groups. It also checks every ordered triple of twists on alphabets of sizes 1 through 3 at lattice sizes 3 and 4. The supplied `verification.json` gives exact counts. These finite checks complement the written all-parameter proofs.

## Formal statement

The declaration `result` in namespace `D5.S3.StatisticalMechanics.ParticleProcesses.SwitchedTwistedSSEP` covers the all-parameter classification, positive sector overlaps and alternating support identities:

https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/blob/6bfc394bbd5147de839dd2116b985b438fcae8cf/D5/S3/StatisticalMechanics/ParticleProcesses/SwitchedTwistedSSEP.lean

The profile-and-charge component classification, its cyclic specialization, the positive-time switching consequence, the finite-family fair-schedule theorem and the uniform limiting law under periodic switching have complete written proofs in the note. The stagewise positive-time support assertion specializes J. R. Norris, Markov Chains (1997), Theorem 3.2.1, p. 111. These statements' scope is distinguished from that declaration in the note's final section.
